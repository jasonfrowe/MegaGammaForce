MEGA GAMMA FORCE
A Radiant Silvergun-style shooter for the Picocomputer 6502.

                 Keyboard       Gamepad
Move             Arrows / WASD  d-pad / left stick
A Vulcan         Z or J         A
B Homing         X or K         B
C Spread         C or L         C or R1
A+B Plasma       V              X
A+C Wide Shot    Z+C            Y
B+C Missiles     B              Z or L1
Radiant Sword    Space          R2
Hyper Sword      Z+X+C          L2
Start / pause    Enter / P      Start

The sword absorbs pink bullets and charges the Hyper Sword.
Destroy three enemies of one color in a row to build the chain;
keep hitting that color to keep it paying, or switch after a full set.
Extra lives at 50,000 and 100,000 points, then every 100,000.
Shoot each boss apart before its core for 100% destruction.
Left and right on the title screen choose the starting stage.
Stages so far: the Sky Fortress, the Neon Spires, the Iron Tide, where
the battleship LEVIATHAN waits, the Scorched Sands, the Frozen Strait,
the Ascent into orbit, the Sea of Tranquility on the Moon and the Deep
Field. Don't fly into the Neon Spires' towers, the Frozen Strait's
icebergs, the satellites or the asteroids. They block most shots and
enemy bullets, but missiles and plasma reach over them.
