MEGA GAMMA FORCE
A Radiant Silvergun-style shooter for the Picocomputer 6502.

                 Keyboard       Gamepad
Move             Arrows / WASD  d-pad / left stick
A Vulcan         Z or J         A
B Homing         X or K         B
C Spread         C or L         C or R1
A+B Plasma       V              X
A+C Wide Shot    Z+C            Y
B+C Homing Sprd  B              Z or L1
Radiant Sword    N or Space     R2
Hyper Sword      M              L2
Start / pause    Enter / P      Start

The sword absorbs pink bullets and charges the Hyper Sword.
Destroy three enemies of one color in a row to build the chain.
Shoot the boss apart before its core for 100% destruction.
